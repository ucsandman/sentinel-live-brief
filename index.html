CFPB finalizes BNPL "credit card" rule — September deadline
The CFPB's interpretive rule treating BNPL lenders as credit card issuers takes effect this quarter. Lenders without billing dispute and refund processes face enforcement exposure. Estimated 40% of current BNPL volume is non-compliant.
